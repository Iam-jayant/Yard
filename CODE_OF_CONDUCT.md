# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Build things. Be straight with people. Don't make this a worse place to be.

---

## Our Standards

SourceCity is a builder-first community. The bar for behavior here is the same as the bar for code: clear intent, honest effort, and respect for the work.

**Expected:**
- Be direct and honest — about your code, your limitations, and your disagreements
- Give feedback on work, not on the person doing it
- Acknowledge when you're wrong or when someone else's approach is better
- Ask questions when you don't understand something instead of assuming
- Keep discussions scoped to the problem at hand

**Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or targeted hostility toward any contributor
- Dismissing someone's contribution because of who they are, not what they built
- Gatekeeping — making contributors feel unwelcome because they're new or less experienced
- Spam, self-promotion outside designated spaces, or off-topic noise
- Any behavior that makes contributors less likely to show up and build

---

## Enforcement

If something violates this code of conduct, report it to Jayant directly (see the repo contact).

**What happens:**
1. The report is reviewed privately
2. If the violation is confirmed, the person is contacted directly and the behavior is addressed
3. Repeated or severe violations result in removal from the project

Reports are handled confidentially. We don't do public callouts.

---

## Scope

This applies to all spaces where SourceCity happens: the repository, issues, PRs, Discord, and any other official channel. It applies to everyone — contributors, maintainers, and visitors alike.
